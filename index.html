🎯 Course Outcomes

CO1 · InferenceApply inferential techniques — estimation, confidence intervals, and hypothesis testing — to real data and interpret the results honestly.
CO2 · Advanced methodsApply and evaluate regression, classification, clustering, and statistical machine learning, with proper validation.
CO3 · PrinciplesExplain the principles, lifecycle, and foundations of data science, from acquisition through deployment and monitoring.
CO4 · Python proficiencyDemonstrate working proficiency in Python and its data science libraries through hands-on Colab practicals.
CO5 · ResearchDesign and execute an original research study on a real dataset, from problem formulation to defensible conclusions.
CO6 · CommunicationCommunicate findings through sound visualization and a conference-format research paper.
Values & ethicsPrivacy, consent, fairness, transparency, and data governance throughout — with RTU's core values: Integrity, Innovativeness, Excellence, Nationalism, Inclusivity.

Grades follow the RTU 1.00–5.00 scale (1.00 highest; 3.00 = 75% minimum passing). A passing capstone mark is required to pass the course. Capstone deadlines are firm — they gate the conference-submission timeline.

🏆 Research Capstone Milestones

Teams of two to four conduct an original study on a real dataset and report it as a full conference paper demonstrating the complete analytics lifecycle.

Week 5 — Team formation & topic registration.Three candidate topics with candidate datasets.
Week 9 — Proposal colloquium.Panel pitch: problem, research questions, dataset, methodology, target conference.
Week 10 — Approved proposal document.Submitted alongside the Midterm Examination.
Week 11 — Data readiness.Acquired and cleaned dataset, data dictionary, ethics screening.
Week 13 — Progress report.Preliminary analysis notebook with EDA and initial models.
Week 14 — Full paper.Camera-ready manuscript, similarity report, internal peer review.
Week 15 — Final defense.Oral defense with a data-storytelling presentation; final paper submission.
